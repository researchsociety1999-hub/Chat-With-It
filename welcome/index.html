Why ChatWithIt

Most AI chat apps ask you to trust their servers.

They hold your API keys and your conversations. ChatWithIt has no server of its own, and you can read every line of the code that shows it.

Typical AI chat app

  • Your key sits on someone else's server
  • Your chats pass through a middleman
  • You rely on a privacy policy

ChatWithIt

  • Your key stays in browser memory for the session
  • Chats go straight to the provider you chose
  • Open source, so you can verify it

The engine

Auditable by design.

Privacy & security

See exactly where your data goes.

No ChatWithIt server ever sees your key or your conversations. Here is the full path, including the parts that aren't perfect.

What stays on your device

Preferences and chat history (7-day expiry) are stored locally. API keys are not stored unless you tap "Save current key" in Profiles.

What we're upfront about

A key saved in Profiles sits unencrypted in this browser's localStorage. Providers have their own data-retention terms, so review OpenRouter's and Hugging Face's policies.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it free?

ChatWithIt is free and MIT licensed, and it lists only free models. You bring your own OpenRouter or Hugging Face key, and the provider's own limits apply.

Do I need an account?

There is no ChatWithIt account because there is no backend. You do need an API key from OpenRouter or Hugging Face.

Where is my API key stored?

In browser memory for the current session. If you use the optional Profiles feature and tap "Save current key", it is saved unencrypted in this browser's localStorage.

Where does my chat history go?

History stays on your device in local storage and expires after 7 days. Messages themselves are sent only to the provider you selected.

Why am I seeing rate-limit errors?

OpenRouter's free tier is limited to roughly 20 requests per minute and 50 per day without credits. Wait a minute, switch to another free model, or add credits on OpenRouter.

Does it work offline?

The installed app shell is cached by a service worker, so the interface loads offline. Chatting still needs an internet connection to reach the model provider.

Which models can I use?

Free models from OpenRouter, plus chat models available through the Hugging Face router. The list refreshes when you add your key.
