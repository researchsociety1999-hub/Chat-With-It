# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in ChatWithIt a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

---

## Our Standards

**Encouraged behavior:**
- Constructive, specific feedback
- Welcoming new contributors
- Focusing on what's best for the project
- Respectful disagreement

**Not acceptable:**
- Personal attacks or insults
- Harassment of any kind
- Dismissive or condescending responses
- Publishing others' private information

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and responded to appropriately.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
